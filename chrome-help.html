Dusk for Chrome: help

Written for Dusk for Chrome 0.3.1. Updated 11 November 2026.

Click the Dusk icon in Chrome’s toolbar to open its popup. If you don’t see it, open Chrome’s Extensions menu (the puzzle piece) and pin Dusk. The problem report shows which version you have.

A site looks wrong

Press Restore this website. Dusk saves Off for that site, and the page, with the frames embedded in it, goes back to its own look. Undo restore brings back the setting you had before.

You can also choose a rule under This site:

  • Follow: the site follows your choice under Website appearance (Follow system, Always dark, Scheduled or Off).
  • Dark or Light: always request that look here, even when Website appearance is Off.
  • Off: Dusk leaves this website untouched.

A rule covers exactly the name shown after This site:, such as www.example.com. Tick Also for all of example.com to make it cover example.com and every name under it, such as docs.example.com; a rule for one exact name still wins over it. Dusk doesn’t offer that box where one domain is shared by many people’s sites, such as github.io, or when it can’t be sure which part is the domain. Your rules are listed under Your site choices; Follow again removes one.

Keyboard shortcut. Control+Shift+D on a Mac (Alt+Shift+N on Windows and Linux) turns Dusk off for the site you are on, like Restore this website, and on again, like Undo restore. Turn Always dark on or off has no key until you give it one. To change either, open chrome://extensions/shortcuts. If another extension already uses the key, Chrome leaves Dusk’s without one.

Page colors chooses how Dusk changes this site:

  • Preserve colors (default) changes grey backgrounds, and the colour of text that would otherwise be hard to read. It aims to preserve photos, videos and coloured areas; results vary by site. If it cannot change a page safely, for example because the page’s whole background is a colour, the page keeps its original look and the popup says so.
  • Standard is a compatibility option for one site. It flips the colours of the whole page, then flips photos, videos and embedded frames back. It may work where Preserve colors can’t, but it can change the colours in pictures. Dusk never switches to Standard by itself.

A page stays dark in Light, or light in Dark

Dusk changes a page only when it shows the opposite of what you asked for, and only where it can do that safely. A site with its own light and dark themes switches by itself, and Dusk leaves its theme alone. Check these in order:

  • Website appearance. Always dark keeps pages dark even when your computer is light. Scheduled makes pages dark between the hours you pick under Dark at and Light at, by your computer’s local time, whatever your computer shows; open tabs switch at those hours, and a tab in the background switches when you go back to it. Off leaves every site alone except those you set to Dark or Light.
  • This site: Dark, Light and Off apply whatever your computer shows. To keep a dark site dark while your computer is light, choose Dark for it.
  • Chrome’s own setting. Follow system follows the light or dark setting Chrome gives websites. If Chrome’s Settings → Appearance → Mode is Light or Dark instead of Device, websites get that, whatever your computer shows. When Chrome is telling websites to be light and the page is unchanged, the popup says so; Try dark on this site sets Dark for that site.
  • The line under Page colors. “Preserve colors can’t change this page” means the page keeps its original look; Standard may work there. “Original appearance restored because controls may be unreadable” means Dusk stepped back; it tries again when you reload the page, switch appearance or change a Dusk setting. For “Page status not verified”, reload the tab once.
  • Things that keep their colour by design. Preserve colors aims to leave photos, videos, embedded video players and coloured areas such as a brand’s banner in their original colours; results vary by site. Dusk changes grey surfaces and text, so some pages stay partly light in Dark. In Light, photo-heavy pages, such as streaming and game landing pages, may stay mostly dark. If Dusk cannot tell whether a page is light or dark, it leaves the page alone rather than guess.
  • Pages no extension can change. Chrome’s own pages, such as Settings and the Chrome Web Store, show “not a supported website” in the popup. PDFs and images open in Chrome’s own viewer are left as they are.

Google Docs and Sheets

Docs draws each page, and Sheets its grid, as a picture rather than with ordinary page colours. With Preserve colors, Dusk darkens that paper along with the rest of the page. A picture inside a document is drawn on the same paper, so it changes colour with it. To see a document exactly as written, choose Off for docs.google.com; that one rule covers Docs, Sheets and Slides.

Incognito

Chrome keeps extensions out of Incognito windows unless you allow it: open chrome://extensions, press Details under Dusk and turn on Allow in Incognito. Dusk then does not remember the sites you visit in those windows. Choices you make in the popup there, such as site rules, are saved and stay after the window closes.

Where your settings live

Your mode (with the hours, for Scheduled), site rules, Page colors choices and remembered sites are stored only in this Chrome profile on this computer. So are up to five dates on which Dusk changed a page (not which page), kept only so the popup can ask once, after a few days of use, “Is Dusk working for you?”. Any answer, or Don’t ask again, stops the question for good. They do not sync to your other computers, and none is sent to Dusk automatically. If you choose to send a problem report by email, it includes the status described below. Remembered sites let a repeat visit get the right look sooner; Forget remembered sites clears them and keeps your site rules. The Chrome Web Store version does not connect to Dusk for Mac: it uses only the choices in its own popup.

Problem reports

Press Preview problem report to see a short report: Dusk’s version, your mode and site rule, how the page looks, which Page colors method is active, and the connection status. The website’s hostname is added only if you tick Include website hostname. The report never holds page content, screenshots or full addresses. Copy report copies it to your clipboard; nothing is sent. Paste it into an email or a GitHub issue (see Contact; issues are public). Report a problem, in the popup’s “Is Dusk working for you?” question, opens an email draft in your own mail app with the same report and no hostname; you decide whether to send it.

If your email app does not open, use Preview problem report → Copy report, then paste it into an email to duskformac@gmail.com. Review it before sending. The privacy policy explains optional support emails and deletion requests.

The one-time question uses up to five local dates when Dusk changed a page, excluding private windows. The dates and whether you answered stay in this browser until you remove the extension; Forget remembered sites clears only remembered appearances.

Uninstall

Right-click the Dusk icon in the toolbar and choose Remove from Chrome, or open chrome://extensions and press Remove under Dusk. Chrome deletes Dusk’s stored settings with it. Reload any tab that was open to see it without Dusk. Chrome then opens a short page on this website with one optional question; Dusk adds nothing to its address.

Contact

Email duskformac@gmail.com, or open an issue on GitHub. Please do not send passwords or other sensitive information.
