Dusk for Mac: App Store Privacy Policy

Effective 12 November 2026. This policy applies only to Dusk for Mac downloaded from the Mac App Store.

The short version

The App Store edition switches your Mac between Light and Dark appearance. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising, or in-app network connection. It does not collect usage data. App downloads and updates are provided by Apple through the App Store.

This policy is separate from the Dusk for Chrome extension and the direct-download Mac edition, which have different features and network behavior. The App Store edition does not install a browser extension.

Information stored on your Mac

Dusk stores its settings locally in a JSON settings file in its own application support folder. These settings include your appearance schedule, the exact shortcut names you enter, verification status for those names, recovery status, and related app preferences. Dusk does not read or change other apps' files, and does not keep an appearance history or app inventory.

Dusk reads system appearance through Apple’s AppKit framework and opens named shortcuts in Apple’s Shortcuts app to change it. Dusk itself does not request the System Events Automation permission. Apple Shortcuts may need it for the optional Compatibility shortcuts, described below. Dusk sends the selected shortcut’s name and, for manual switching and verification, a temporary callback identifier through a local Shortcuts URL. Dusk does not send documents, clipboard content, or account information as shortcut input.

Apple Shortcuts

Basic setup uses the two shortcuts described in our support instructions, each containing one fixed Set Appearance action. Dusk cannot inspect or cancel a user-selected shortcut. If you add other actions, the shortcut may access data or external services under its own permissions. Those external workflows and Apple’s Shortcuts services are separate from Dusk and are governed by their own privacy practices.

Optional Compatibility shortcuts

Optional Compatibility uses either prepared shortcut imports or the same fixed appearance scripts set up by hand under Troubleshoot. Both run through Apple Shortcuts and System Events, and require Allow Running Scripts in Shortcuts. macOS may also ask you to allow Shortcuts to control System Events. Dusk does not change those permissions or read the contents of your shortcut library. Basic setup remains available using Apple's built-in Set Appearance actions.

Report a problem

Report a problem creates a local preview containing your descriptions of what happened and what you expected, plus seven coarse Dusk/system status lines. Nothing is sent automatically. Copy report writes the preview to your clipboard at your request. Open email draft opens your email app; if you choose to send it, we receive your email address, any sender name included by your email app, and the report to respond to your request. Review your description before sending and do not include sensitive information. Dusk does not automatically attach shortcut names, shortcut contents, logs, screenshots, browsing history or clipboard contents.

Retention and deletion

Your local settings remain on your Mac until you remove them. Uninstalling the app may leave those settings behind. To reset them, quit Dusk, open Finder → Go → Go to Folder, enter ~/Library/Containers/dev.calvin.dusk.store/Data/Library/Application Support/DuskStore/, and move only settings.json to the Trash. Reopening Dusk starts setup again with Auto paused. This does not delete your Apple Shortcuts; you manage those separately in Shortcuts. We cannot access or delete your local settings remotely.

If you email us for support, we use your address, any sender name, and message only to investigate and respond. We retain unresolved support correspondence while working on it and delete resolved support correspondence from our support mailbox, including Trash, within 90 days after resolution. We do not sell support correspondence or use it for advertising.

You can request earlier deletion, or withdraw permission for further support contact, by emailing monomadstudio@gmail.com from the address you used. We will delete the associated support messages and stop contacting you, apart from confirming your request. Deletion does not remove copies in your own email account or copies retained independently by your email provider under its policies. No account registration or identity documents are needed.

Network and hosting

The app itself makes no network connections. Opening Privacy policy launches this website in your browser; sending a support email uses your email app and provider. We use Gmail for support mail, subject to Google’s Privacy Policy. Apple handles App Store delivery and updates under Apple's own policies. This support and privacy website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub says it logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes; see the GitHub Pages information and GitHub General Privacy Statement for details about the host's data practices.

Email support

If you email monomadstudio@gmail.com, we receive your email address, any sender name included by your email app, and whatever information you choose to include, and use it to respond to your request. Email is voluntary. We do not receive information through the app itself.

Children

Dusk does not collect information through the app, including from children.

Changes and contact

We will update the effective date when this policy changes. Questions about this policy can be sent to monomadstudio@gmail.com.
