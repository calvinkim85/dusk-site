Dusk for Mac: App Store Support

For the Dusk edition downloaded from the Mac App Store. Updated 6 October 2026.

What this edition does

Dusk switches your Mac between Light and Dark appearance. You can choose a mode yourself or set a daily schedule. This App Store edition controls macOS appearance only. It does not install the Dusk for Chrome extension or the separate direct-download edition.

Set up Apple Shortcuts

This edition requires macOS 14 or later and Apple’s Shortcuts app. In Shortcuts, create two shortcuts with distinct names. Each must contain exactly one built-in Set Appearance action: set one to Light and the other to Dark. Do not add other actions.

Open Shortcut setup… from Dusk’s menu-bar popup. Enter and save the exact names. With your Mac in Light mode, verify the Dark shortcut. Then, with your Mac in Dark mode, verify the Light shortcut. Dusk checks the response from Shortcuts and the actual appearance change. Both shortcuts must be verified before Auto is available.

Dusk opens the shortcuts you name; it cannot inspect or cancel their actions. Use only the two simple appearance shortcuts described above. If you rename or edit them in Shortcuts, check their actions and verify the correct names again in Dusk. Shortcuts is a separate Apple app and may show its own interface or messages.

Scheduling and manual mode

Choose Light or Dark for a manual change, or set your daily hours and choose Auto. A manual choice pauses Auto. The schedule runs while Dusk is open and your Mac is awake; after waking, Dusk evaluates the current scheduled mode. Turn on Open at login if you want Dusk to start when you sign in.

Scheduled changes use a Shortcuts URL and observe the requested appearance. This observation does not confirm that a shortcut finished or caused the change. If a change cannot be confirmed, Auto pauses and will not retry automatically.

If a shortcut fails or takes too long

Open Shortcuts, inspect or stop the shortcut, and check your Mac’s current appearance. An operation may still be running after Dusk times out. Follow the recovery instructions in Dusk before trying again. Check that the saved names exactly match the two shortcuts and that each has only the fixed Set Appearance action. If you change a saved name, verify it again before resuming Auto.

Avoid competing appearance schedules

Set System Settings → Appearance to Light or Dark when using Dusk’s Auto schedule. macOS Auto and other appearance utilities can otherwise change the mode independently. Apps with fixed themes must be configured to follow the system separately. Dusk does not recolor website content or images.

Contact support

Email duskformac@gmail.com. Tell us what happened and which macOS version you use. Email is voluntary: if you contact us, we receive the email address and any information you include so we can reply. Please do not send passwords or other sensitive information.
